Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 3, 2025

At Galorem AI, your privacy is a top priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal and academic data when you use our web-based AI study assistant. By using Galorem AI, you agree to the practices outlined below.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of data to enhance your experience and improve our services:

  • Personal Information: Your name, email address, and optionally, profile photo (if account registration is used).
  • Academic Documents: Files you upload (e.g., PDFs, Word docs) are processed by our AI to extract summaries, questions, and study guides.
  • Usage Data: Interactions with features (e.g., number of uploads, clicks, timestamps) to improve functionality.
  • Device & Technical Data: Browser type, OS, IP address, screen resolution, and access times.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate intelligent summaries and answers based on your uploaded content.
  • To personalize your experience with recent files and saved learning progress.
  • To improve system performance, fix bugs, and analyze feature engagement.
  • To provide technical support and send service-related updates (if applicable).

3. AI and Data Handling

Galorem AI uses machine learning models to process your academic materials. These documents are analyzed temporarily and securely, and are not used to retrain or feed any external AI systems. No human reviews your files unless you request support or feedback.

4. Cookies and Analytics

We use cookies and local storage to remember your preferences, login sessions, and page interactions. Basic analytics are used to understand usage patterns, feature popularity, and potential improvements. No third-party ad networks are used.

5. Data Storage and Security

All uploaded content is stored securely using encrypted storage methods. We implement strict access controls, HTTPS encryption, and session management to protect your data from unauthorized access or misuse.

6. User Accounts (If Enabled)

When account registration is enabled, users may create profiles to access saved content and personalized features. You can request account deletion and data removal at any time by emailing us at support@galorem.app.

7. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers. Limited services such as email support or analytics may use third-party providers, but only under strict data protection agreements.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we store about you
  • Request corrections or deletions
  • Withdraw consent and disable cookies
  • Request a copy of your data (data portability)

9. Children's Privacy

Galorem AI is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children, and any such data will be deleted upon discovery.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. All changes will be posted on this page with the updated date. We encourage you to review it regularly.
